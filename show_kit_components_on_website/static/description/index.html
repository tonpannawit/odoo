Products with Bills of Materials

Products that use Bills of Materials (BOM) can be sold as Kits or Manufactured items. In the example below, the product "Table Kit" contains multiple components defined in its BOM.

BOM Details Made Visible

When a product has a BOM, your website customers will be able to see the full breakdown of components— just like you see inside Odoo. This transparency helps users understand exactly what is included in a Kit or Manufactured product.

Clear Website Display

Each component is displayed with its product image, name, and quantity required per kit. This eliminates unnecessary questions and reduces support emails or calls about what’s included.

Clear information = higher customer confidence and faster purchases.

Upselling & Cross-Selling Built-In

If a component product is published on your website, its name becomes a clickable link. Customers can easily browse to the component’s product page and add it to their cart — increasing upselling opportunities with zero extra effort.
